I spend two focused hours every day on Python, LLM applications, tool use, workflows and evaluation, and I'm turning that practice into working projects. Evaluation experience is a strong base for this: knowing how models fail is half of building agents that don't.

How I Work

The habits that make my labels reliable

Consistent judgments

I apply the rubric the same way on the hundredth task as on the first, and I flag guidelines that are ambiguous instead of guessing.

Edge cases first

Models fail at the boundaries. I look for the hard cases, document them, and help tighten the guidelines that cover them.

Rationales that hold up

I write short, specific reasons for each ranking, so a reviewer can see exactly why one output beat another.

Reviewer's eye

Years of quality audits and reviewing other annotators' work taught me to catch the errors others miss, and to explain them kindly.

Measure and improve

Six Sigma habits: define the standard, measure where work drifts from it, fix the root cause and track the result.
